# aisle-march-2020.jpg

**What it is.** The lunch-meat aisle of a Washington, DC grocery store on 18 March 2020, five days
after the US national-emergency declaration, with most shelves emptied by panic buying.

**Author.** Kurt Kaiser. **License.** CC0 1.0 (public-domain dedication) — no attribution
required; credited on the slide anyway.
**Source.** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Grocery_store_at_lunch_meat_aisle_empty_Corona_virus_2020.jpg
**Retrieved.** 2026-10-13, downscaled from 4608×3456 to 2000 px wide for the slides.

**Why this photo.** Lecture 8's convolution examples run on it. It has the strong lines an edge
filter needs — shelf edges, price tags — and it is a forecasting picture: the shelves are empty
because demand forecasts missed the March 2020 shock, the lesson the course closes on in Week 16.
